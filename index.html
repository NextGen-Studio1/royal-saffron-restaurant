Our Culinary Heritage

A Symphony of Flavors & Royal Traditions

Born from a passion for India’s grand royal dastarkhwans, Royal Saffron pays homage to the legendary feast traditions of Awadhi Nawabs, Rajput Maharajas, and Mughal Emperors.

Our culinary philosophy centers on patience, uncompromised ingredient authenticity, and precision spice layering. We never use artificial essences or commercial short-cuts — each gravy is slow-braised to perfection.

“Every grain of saffron, every crackle of cumin in pure ghee, and every hour of dum cooking is a reverence to ancient royal kitchens.”
Chef Ranveer Mehra
